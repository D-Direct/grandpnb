Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through our website, we collect the following information:

  • Name and email address
  • Phone number (optional)

  • Travel dates and preferences
  • Any additional information you choose to share about your travel plans

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your travel inquiries

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your information is only shared with:

  • Our team members who need access to assist with your travel inquiry

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

6. Cookies

Our website does not use tracking cookies or analytics. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum.
